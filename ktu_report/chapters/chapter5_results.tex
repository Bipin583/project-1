\section{Statistical Significance Testing}
Paired Wilcoxon Signed-Rank tests demonstrate that ConfTest's failure recall improvements over heuristic and uncalibrated baselines are statistically significant at $\alpha = 0.05$ ($p < 0.00001$). Cliff's delta non-parametric effect sizes range from medium ($\delta = 0.3874$) to large ($\delta = 1.0000$).

\section{Feature Ablation Findings}
Leave-One-Group-Out (LOGO) experiments reveal that Historical Telemetry ($\Delta\text{PR-AUC} = -0.1171$) and Static Call-Graph Coupling ($\Delta\text{PR-AUC} = -0.0837$) provide the highest predictive signal for regression failure detection.

\section{Flakiness Robustness}
Under $10\%$ injected label noise, ConfTest's sample downweighting mechanism maintained a $20.0\%$ failure recall advantage over naive unweighted gradient boosting models ($60.0\%$ vs $40.0\%$).
